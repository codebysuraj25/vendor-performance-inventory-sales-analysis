# 📊 Vendor Performance, Inventory & Sales Analysis

A Data Analytics project focused on analyzing vendor performance, sales trends, inventory management, and profitability using Python, SQL, and Business Intelligence tools.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail and wholesale business data to identify high-performing vendors, understand inventory patterns, evaluate purchasing behavior, and improve business profitability.

The analysis combines data processing, exploratory data analysis, SQL-based data integration, and business intelligence visualization to generate actionable insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze vendor sales and purchase contributions.
- Identify profitable and low-performing brands.
- Evaluate inventory turnover and unsold inventory.
- Analyze the relationship between bulk purchasing and unit prices.
- Understand vendor dependency and purchasing concentration.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 1. Vendor Performance Analysis
Analyzes vendor sales, purchase contribution, and profitability to identify key suppliers.

### 2. Inventory Analysis
Identifies slow-moving inventory and estimates capital tied up in unsold products.

### 3. Brand Performance Analysis
Identifies brands with comparatively low sales and high profit margins.

### 4. Bulk Purchasing Analysis
Evaluates the relationship between purchase quantity and average unit purchase prices.

### 5. Exploratory Data Analysis
Uses statistical analysis and visualization to understand patterns, correlations, and outliers.

### 6. Statistical Hypothesis Testing
Compares the profit margins of high-performing and low-performing vendors using statistical testing.

## 📈 Key Findings

The project reports the following results:

- Total Sales: $441.41 million
- Total Purchase: $307.34 million
- Gross Profit: $134.07 million
- Profit Margin: 38.72%
- Unsold Capital: $2.71 million
- Top 10 Vendors' Purchase Contribution: 65.69%

These findings are based on the project's supplied datasets and analysis.
